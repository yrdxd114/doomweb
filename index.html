Avengers: Doomsday

Doctor Doom arrives, and heroes from three universes, the Avengers and Wakandans, the X-Men and the Fantastic Four, have to stand together against him. It is the first Avengers film in seven years and sets up the Multiverse Saga finale, Secret Wars.

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. Robert Downey Jr. plays Doom. PG-13, 165 minutes.
